

The first modification in the development of the "minicat-battle" chassis involved redesigning the model in Fusion 360 to adapt the fasteners to components more readily available in our local market. To achieve this, the holes in the original .stl file were modified to replace the M3 bolts and nuts with M4s.

However, after initial printing tests, it was discovered that increasing the hole diameter critically reduced the wall thickness in the steering and drive axle areas, weakening their structural resistance to mechanical stress.

To resolve this issue without sacrificing the advantage of the available hardware, a hybrid design was adopted: M4 bolts were retained in the overall vehicle structure, but M3 components were kept exclusively on the steering and drive axles to preserve their geometric integrity. Additionally, the drive axle thickness was increased, and the rear axle support was reinforced, resulting in a much more robust and stable final prototype.
